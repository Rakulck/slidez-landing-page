## Summary

Slidez's organic search performance more than doubled in the last 28 days versus the prior period, driven almost entirely by non-branded content discovery — a strong signal that the blog/content SEO strategy is working. The core constraint right now is **ranking depth, not content coverage**: the vast majority of non-branded queries sit on page 3-5, which mechanically caps CTR regardless of copywriting quality. The two highest-leverage moves are (1) pushing `/blog/best-ai-stylist-apps` — already the site's #1 impression driver — from position ~17 into the top 10, and (2) closing the US market's position/CTR gap, since the US alone represents nearly half of all impressions but underperforms every other major market. No destructive or urgent problems were found: there is no page-level traffic loss and only minor keyword cannibalization.

No changes have been made to the website. This report is analysis only.
